Process

From API call to payment
in three steps

No complex setup. Create invoices programmatically, share a hosted checkout page, and get paid.

01

Create an invoice

POST to /v1/invoices with amount, currency, and customer details. Get a checkout_url back instantly.

02

Share the checkout link

Your customer follows the link to a hosted checkout page on your custom domain or your slug.saftaja.xyz subdomain.

03

Get paid

Customer pays via card with full 3DS authentication or Apple Pay. Funds are processed through your MPGS gateway account.

Features

Everything you need
to accept payments

API-first

Every action is available via REST API. Build invoicing into any workflow, backend, or automation.

Custom domains

Host checkout on your own domain. On-demand TLS via Caddy. Your brand, not ours.

Hosted checkout

A complete, themeable payment page. No frontend work required — share the URL and you're done.

3DS authentication

Full 3D Secure flow built-in. Compliant authentication with Mastercard and Visa card schemes.

MPGS integration

Direct integration with Mastercard Payment Gateway Services — the standard for MENA banks.

Multi-tenant
